## 📖 Overview 

My Activity Planner App is a personal productivity tool that allows users to seamlessly plan out their days, manage pending activities, and stay on top of deadlines. Built with a focus on user experience and efficiency, this application ensures you never miss an important task.

## ✨ Key Features

*   **Daily & Weekly Views:** Easily visualize your schedule at a glance.
*   **Task Prioritization:** Categorize tasks by urgency and importance.
*   **Progress Tracking:** Mark activities as pending, in progress, or completed.
*   **Reminders & Notifications:** Stay alerted about upcoming deadlines.
*   **Clean Interface:** A distraction-free environment for focused planning.

## 💡 How to Use This App

This application is designed to be user-friendly for everyone. Here is exactly what each person needs to do to start organizing their life:

### 1. Account Setup
*   **Sign Up / Log In:** Open the app and create a secure account or log in with your existing credentials. This ensures your data is saved and synced.

### 2. Adding Your Activities
*   **Create a Task:** Click the **"+" (Add New)** button on the main dashboard.
*   **Provide Details:** Enter a clear title for your activity, a brief description, and the due date/time.
*   **Set Priority:** Choose the priority level (e.g., High, Medium, Low) so you know what needs your attention first.
*   **Save:** Click "Save" to add the activity to your planner.

### 3. Managing Your Day
*   **Review Your Dashboard:** Check your daily list every morning to see what needs to be done.
*   **Update Status:** As you work, change the status of your tasks from *Pending* to *In Progress*. 
*   **Complete Tasks:** Once you finish an activity, mark it as *Done*. Enjoy the satisfaction of clearing your list!

### 4. Customization (Optional but Recommended)
*   **Edit or Delete:** Made a mistake? Tap on any existing task to edit its details or delete it entirely if it's no longer relevant.
*   **Filter & Sort:** Use the filter options to view only High Priority tasks or tasks due today.
